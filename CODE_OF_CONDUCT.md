# Code of conduct

We want a community where people can contribute regardless of experience,
background, identity, disability, or language.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people. Explain disagreements with evidence.
- Welcome newcomers and help make discussions understandable.
- Respect privacy, consent, and other contributors' time.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work to repair their impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and retaliation against reporters are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

The current maintainer is [@john-tavares](https://github.com/john-tavares). For a
non-sensitive moderation request, mention the maintainer in the relevant discussion.
For a sensitive report, use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. If none is available, open an issue requesting a private contact
channel without including incident details or personal information. Do not post
confidential reports publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers assess reports in context and handle private information discreetly.
They may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or
ban a participant depending on severity and repeated behavior. Explain decisions
to affected participants when safe to do so. Reporters must not face retaliation.

To appeal a decision, contact the maintainer through the same reporting route and
provide relevant context. This policy does not promise a fixed response time.
